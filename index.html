Got Questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know before joining Impact Self Defense Academy in Agra.

Ask Us Directly
Why should I learn karate?

Karate builds physical strength, coordination, balance and stamina while training your mind for discipline, confidence and self-awareness — making every area of life easier.

How much does it cost?

Training is completely free of charge. We believe quality martial arts should be accessible to every family in Agra — no fees, ever.

Do I need prior experience to join?

Absolutely not. Most students start with zero experience. Our Sensei guides you through every step from your very first class.

How old should my child be?

Age 8 is ideal. Some 6–7 year olds with good focus may join after a short try-out session with the Sensei to assess readiness.

How often should I practice?

At least 1 hour per week outside class — just 10 minutes a day. The more consistent you are, the faster you progress through belt levels.

What do I call the teacher?

Instructors are addressed as "Sensei" — a Japanese title meaning "teacher." It reflects the deep culture of martial arts we uphold.
