Frequently Asked Questions

The benefits of Karate practice are huge! Karate can increase your physical strength, coordination, balance, flexibility and stamina. Whatever you do in your life becomes a little easier because of karate. Karate not only trains your body but also trains your mind! It improves self-awareness, discipline, confidence and will power. Not only will you become more confident that you can defend yourself, but your confidence will increase to deal with all of life's trials.

We don't take any charge for it. We are providing it absolutely free.

All students are required to wear a karate uniform (Gi) to class. Students will need to have a plain white t-shirt under their uniform top!

ABSOLUTELY NOT! Most of our new students do not have any experience, and that is 100% fine! We'll be able to help you along every step of the way to your success!

Age 8 seems about the perfect age to start karate. Students younger than 8 years old need to 'Try Out' to see if Karate is a good fit for them. We do have a few 6 and 7 year old kids that have shown enough maturity. The style we do for Self Defense is sometimes just not possible to teach a 6 and 7 year old in an effective manner.

Whenever you have time! We suggest setting aside at least 1 hour per week for practicing outside of class — that is only 10 minutes per day! Obviously the more you practice, the better you will become.

Most of us come to karate in order to become more coordinated and physically fit. So the brief answer is "not very". Even if you feel like the world's biggest klutz you can still practice karate and your coordination and fitness will greatly improve.

Instructors are addressed as "Sensei". Sensei is a Japanese title of respect (sort of like "Sir" or "Mister"). It means "teacher" and is considered very improper to refer to oneself as Sensei or Master.
